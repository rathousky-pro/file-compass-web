File Compass is a desktop file manager for Windows, developed by Jan Rathouský under the Rathousky.pro name. Work with local folders, archives, network locations and your own cloud accounts in a two-panel interface.

Manage files

Browse, copy, move, rename and search files. Preview documents and source code, work with archives, and analyze folder sizes.

Connect storage

Connect to network servers and optionally link Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Each person signs in with their own account.

Google Drive integration

File Compass uses Google authorization in your browser and rclone as a local helper. With your permission, it can list, read, download, upload, create, rename, move and delete files and folders in your Google Drive. Full Drive access is needed to browse and manage existing folders, not just files created by this app.

Your Google password is entered only on Google's sign-in page. The publisher does not receive your password, authorization tokens or Drive files. See our Privacy Policy for storage, deletion and revocation details.

Availability: File Compass is in development. Public Google OAuth verification is not yet complete. No public installer is currently offered on this website.
